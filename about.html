ABOUT

AT THE CROSS LIVE is a program that aims to minister the power of the Cross of Jesus Christ to those who are lost and hurting.

The program airs on Bible Discovery TV, YouTube and Facebook every Saturday from 11:00 pm to 1:00 am (EST). Join Corville and other guest hosts as they set to inspire and to reach out a helping hand to anyone who is seeking and in need!

Remember, 1 Corinthians 1:18, "The message of the Cross … Is the power of God."

What We Believe

Mission Statement

"AT THE CROSS LIVE" is a vehicle for serving lost and hurting souls with the message of the Cross of Jesus Christ through interactive media.

Vision Statement

The vision of "AT THE CROSS LIVE" is to be motivated by God's love, to serve the hurting and lost souls in their darkest hour of need.

Core Values

These "Core Values" are the fundamental principles and objectives that lie at the centre of "AT THE CROSS LIVE".

1. We Are Christian

We believe in the inerrancy of the word of God and the power of His love demonstrated by Encouragement, Hope, Comfort, Love and Salvation through the Cross of Jesus Christ. 1 Corinthians 1:18, "For the message of the cross is … the power of God."

2. We Are Committed to Pointing People to Christ

We believe Jesus Christ is the answer to all that ails mankind. John 14:6, "I am the way and the truth and the life."

3. We Focus on Issues That Matter

We believe God cares about what concerns us. Therefore we will have a different focus issue each week addressing a real life concern. 1 Peter 5:7, "Cast all your anxiety on him because he cares for you."

4. We Are Committed to Good Stewardship

We believe in being prudent with the resources God makes available, to see lives transformed through the "Good News" of Jesus Christ. 1 Corinthians 4:2, "It is required that those who have been given a trust must prove faithful."

Statement of Faith

We believe people's lives can be radically changed experiencing new life, hope and healing through having a personal relationship with Jesus and that salvation from sin and all that ails mankind only comes through Jesus Christ, the Son of God. We believe in the fullness of the Holy Spirit who empowers us to live Godly lives.
